Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Guess The Number ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play. Please read this carefully.

01 Information We Do Not Collect

Guess The Number does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal data to use the App.

We do not collect personal data. The App works fully offline for its core gameplay.

02 Advertising (Unity Ads)

The App uses Unity Ads to display advertisements, including banner ads and interstitial ads. Unity Ads may collect certain data to serve relevant advertisements, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Device type, operating system, and language settings
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Unity Technologies under their own Privacy Policy. You can review it at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

03 Data Storage

The App stores minimal data locally on your device only (such as game preferences). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

04 Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since we do not collect any personal data ourselves, we believe the App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

Unity Ads provides a child-directed mode. If you believe your child has been exposed to inappropriate data collection, please contact us immediately.

05 Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party service:

  • Unity Ads — advertising platform by Unity Technologies. Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services. We encourage you to review their policies.

06 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data held about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Since we do not collect personal data directly, most of these rights apply to data held by Unity Technologies. Please refer to their privacy policy to exercise those rights.

07 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
